# BOM
- 1 Battery
- 1 Battery Switch
- 1 unpolarized capacitor
- 1 .1 uF capacitor
- 1 10 uF capacitor
- 2 1.5k resistor
- 4 330 ohm resistors
- 1 100K ohm potentiometer
- 1 NE555D IC
- 1 CD4017 decade counter IC
